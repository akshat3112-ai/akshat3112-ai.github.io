Cutting Chai: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Cutting Chai ("the game") is made by Perfect Fifth. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to do that. Your best scores and settings stay on your phone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of full-screen ads, served by Google AdMob:

  • an optional "Second chance" video you can choose to watch for more time in a shift, and
  • an occasional ad between shifts.

There are no banner ads. When the game requests or shows an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version), and how you interact with ads (for example, whether you tapped one),
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs and ad performance.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices (see below), ads may be personalised based on your interests or non-personalised. Cutting Chai does not receive or store this information itself.

More from Google: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices about ads and consent

  • UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to the use of your data for ads, including personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings (the gear on the menu) → "Privacy & ads settings".
  • Everyone: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).
  • If you play without an internet connection, no ads are requested or shown.

What stays on your phone

To work properly, the game keeps a small file on your own phone with:

  • your best score and the furthest glass you've reached,
  • which tips and instructions you've already seen,
  • your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, screen shake, volumes),
  • how many shifts you've played and when the game last asked you to rate it.

This file never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Rating the game

After a good shift the game may show Google Play's own rating card. Anything you write there is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the game is not told whether or how you rated.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for short vibrations when you pour, zap a fly and so on. You can switch vibration off in the game.
  • Internet and network state: used only by Google AdMob to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above.

The game asks for no other permissions that need your approval. Google's ads SDK also lists a few routine system permissions (such as keeping the device awake while an ad video loads); none of them give access to your contacts, photos, location or files.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Challenge a friend", the game opens WhatsApp (or your browser) with a message about your score already typed. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and whatever you send is handled by that app under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them. It does not knowingly collect information from children.
