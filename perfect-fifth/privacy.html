Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Perfect Fifth ("the app") is an Android app for practising ABRSM Grade 5 music theory, made by Perfect Fifth. This policy explains what information is used when you use it.

In short: the app keeps your progress on your phone and sends nothing to us. From version 1.1.0 it shows occasional ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to do that. Ads are never personalised.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps these on your own phone, in its private storage:

  • the first name you enter (optional, used only to greet you),
  • your practice history, topic scores, readiness, streak and achievements,
  • your mock exam results and the questions you answered, so you can review them,
  • your settings, and small counters such as which days you used the app and when it last asked you to rate it.

This never leaves your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. You can erase it in the app (Settings → Reset all progress), and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob), from version 1.1.0

Version 1.1.0 and later show an occasional full-screen ad served by Google AdMob, only at natural breaks: after a set of practice questions, after a Music in Context set, or on the mock exam results screen. There are never ads during a mock exam, in the middle of a set of questions, during the first-run quick check, or when the app opens. There are no banner ads. Earlier versions contain no ads.

The ads are set up for a young audience:

  • every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent;
  • ads are never personalised: they are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps;
  • only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are allowed.

When the app requests or shows an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes some information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version), and how you interact with ads (for example, whether you tapped one),
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs and ad performance.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Perfect Fifth does not receive or store this information itself.

More from Google: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices about ads and consent

  • UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland: before any ad is shown, Google's consent form asks for your choice about the use of your data for ads. No ad is loaded until this is done.
  • Everyone: you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).
  • Without an internet connection, no ads are requested or shown, and the app works as normal.

Rating the app

After a good result (for example a strong practice set or passing a mock exam), the app may occasionally show Google Play's own rating card: at most once a month and never more than three times. Anything you write there is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the app is not told whether or how you rated it.

Reporting a question

If you tap "Report this question", the app opens your own email app with the question's details filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

Sound

The app plays notes and chords from recordings built into the app. It does not use the microphone and does not record anything.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state (from version 1.1.0): used only by Google AdMob to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: not used. The app removes the advertising ID permission, and ads in this app are never personalised, as described above.

The app asks for no permissions that need your approval, and has no access to your contacts, photos, location or files.

Children

Music theory students of any age, including children, may use the app. The app does not ask for any personal information except an optional first name, which stays on the phone. Ads are requested as directed to children, are never personalised, and are limited to general-audience content.
